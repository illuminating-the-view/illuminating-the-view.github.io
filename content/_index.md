Welcome to Illuminating the View, a site containing original long form essays on Buddhism. These
essays are authored with the purpose of deepening my own understanding, providing quality
information on Buddhism, and translating said essays into Spanish, a language lacking content on the
subject.

The essays are informed to the best of my ability by the understanding preserved in the aural
lineages of which I am part. My bias is towards traditional understandings of Buddhism as opposed to
modernist interpretations which have been given more prominence outside of Asia than they should
have. I confess my mistakes and shortcomings to the Three Jewels in advance, hoping that at least a
part of this site will help others navigate the large body of Buddhist teachings and help them
connect with a qualified teacher.
